#math #definition
Given two differentiable [[manifold|manifolds]] $M, N$, a continuously differentiable map $f: M \to N$ is called a **diffeomorphism** if $f$ is bijective and $f^{-1}$ is also continuously differentiable. 


Note: In our geometry course, diffeomorphism usually means $C^\infty$.

See also: [[Homeomorphism]]
